# Getting Started with Habita

A short guide to creating your account, logging in, and getting back in if you forget your password.

> **What's available today:** you can create an account, confirm your email, log in and out, and reset your password. The rest of Habita is on its way and will appear after you log in.

---

## 1. Create your account

1. Open Habita and select **Create an account**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**
   - **Email**: use an address you can open right now. We'll send you a message.
   - **Password**: at least 12 characters, with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, and a number.
   - **Confirm password**: type the same password again.
3. Tick **I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy**. You need to do this to continue.
4. Select **Create account**.

You'll see a **Check your email** screen.

**If something goes wrong:**

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Enter your name." / "Enter a valid email address." | Fix the field shown. |
| "Passwords do not match." | Retype the confirmation. |
| "This email is already in use." | You may already have an account. Go to **Log in**, or use **Forgot password?** (section 4). |
| "Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to continue." | Tick the box. |
| "Something went wrong. Please try again." | Wait a moment and try again. Your answers are kept. |

## 2. Confirm your email

We email you a link titled **Verify your Habita email**. You must open it before you can log in.

1. Open the email and select **Verify email**.
2. You arrive on the Log in page with **"Email verified. You can log in now."**

Tips:

- The link works for **24 hours**. Opening it twice does no harm.
- Can't find the email? Check your spam folder. On the Check your email screen, select **Resend email** ("Did not get it?"). You'll see "Verification email sent again."
- If the link has expired you'll see "This verification link has expired. Log in to request a new one." Try to log in; you'll be offered **Resend email**.

## 3. Log in and out

1. Select **Log in**, then enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Tick **Remember me** if this is your own device. You'll stay logged in for up to 30 days. Without it, you stay logged in for a shorter time while you're using the site, and then you'll be asked to log in again.
3. Select **Log in**.

To leave, select **Log out**.

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "These credentials do not match our records." | Check your email and password. Use **Forgot password?** if needed. |
| "Verify your email before logging in." with **Resend email** | Your account isn't confirmed yet. Select **Resend email** and open the new link. |
| "Your session has expired. Please log in again." | You were logged out for security. Log in again. |
| A message about too many attempts | Wait a minute and try again. |
| A message that your account is deactivated | Your account has been switched off. Contact Habita support. |

> **Using a shared computer?** Leave **Remember me** unticked and always select **Log out** when you're done.

## 4. Forgot your password

1. On the Log in page select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your **Email** and select **Send reset link**.
3. You'll see **Check your email**. For your privacy, Habita shows the same message whether or not the address has an account.
4. Open the email titled **Reset your Habita password** and select **Reset password**.
5. Type your new password twice and select **Update password**.
6. You'll see **Password updated**. Select **Continue to log in**.

Good to know:

- The reset link works for **60 minutes** and **only once**. Asking for a new link cancels the old one.
- If you see **This link has expired**, select **Request a new link**.
- After a reset, you're signed out everywhere. Log in again with the new password on each device.
- If you didn't ask for a reset, ignore the email. Your password stays the same.
- A reset also confirms your email address, so it's a handy way in if the verification email went missing.

## 5. Keeping your account safe

- Use a password you don't use anywhere else.
- Habita will **never** ask for your password in an email.
- Our emails never include your name, and they only ever link to Habita.
- If you suspect someone else knows your password, use **Forgot password?** straight away.

## 6. Need help?

If an email never arrives after a couple of tries, or your account is deactivated, contact Habita support.
